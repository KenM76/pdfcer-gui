## Why it is in this repo at all

Ken, 2026-09-04:

> *"you should also try zooming in on the atoms of the banana pdf file and see
> what happens when you try to draw a box around a molecule and move it, or
> select the ion and move it, or edit the nodes at that scale. you should check

★★★ **It is a genuine bug-finding instrument, not a toy.** A banana at life
size, two of its cells at that same scale, and **eight tiers of zoom ending at a
10 nm ATP synthase particle readable at about 35,000,000 %**. The engine's own
notes record it finding three real defects in one session — forms executed
off-screen, an inverted unit constant, and a self-crossing subpath that stroked
perfectly and filled wrong.

⇒ For this shell it is the only fixture that crosses **every** rendering tier in
one document: `Strategy::WholePage`, `Strategy::Region`, and the deep-zoom path
that runs to 10¹² %. A defect in hit-testing, grip placement or the
click-to-page coordinate conversion that only appears above a threshold has
nowhere to hide in it.

★★ **Verify by rendering, not by reading.** All three of the engine's defects
produced plausible-looking output; the self-crossing one needed a
**160,000,000 %** render of a single crista to see at all. That is the same rule
this project holds for layout — one oracle, and it is a rendered image.

⚠ **It contains a personal easter egg** — a heart of mitochondria inside the
pulp cell's vacuole. Do not "simplify" anything here, and if a change of ours
appears to break it, the bug is ours.
