# `ui-verify/checks/shrink_pictures`

`shrink_pictures_reaches_every_selected_picture` — **two pictures gathered
with Ctrl+click reach Format ▸ Make pictures smaller together, from the Format
tab and from a right-click.**

## What it drives

Opens `fixtures/two-photos.pdf` (two 400×400 grey noise pictures, each its
own image XObject, drawn 96 pt square: 300 ppi) in Edit mode.

1. Click picture A, Ctrl+click picture B; require `canvas-selection sel=2
   level=Object`.
2. Press `ribbon.item.format.shrink_images`; require
   `shrink-images-opened pictures=2`. Press `shrink-images.commit`; require
   `images-shrunk asked=2 changed=2 resized=2`.
3. Gather both again, right-click picture B, press
   `menu.item.canvas.object.format.shrink_images`; require
   `shrink-images-opened pictures=2`, then after Apply `images-shrunk asked=2`.
   The pictures are already at 150 ppi by then, so this pass may change
   nothing; what it proves is that the right-click kept both selected and
   handed both to the engine.

## What a failure means

| arm | failure |
|---|---|
| gather | Ctrl+click does not add to the selection |
| opened | the route is missing, or opened on fewer than both pictures (a right-click that replaced the selection reads `pictures=1`) |
| shrunk | Apply never reached the engine, or the plan rewrote fewer than both |

Generated by `fixtures/two-photos.PROVENANCE.py`; noise, because a flat
picture scaled down compresses worse and the engine rightly keeps it.
