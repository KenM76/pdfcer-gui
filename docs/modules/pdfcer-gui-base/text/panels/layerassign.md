# `text::panels::layerassign`

The words of Move to layer: the Properties panel's Layer row (heading, *No
layer*, *Several layers*, *A visibility rule*, and the hover text for a
selection of parts or across pages), the Move to layer window, and the two
receipts. `objects_moved` names the moved and already-there counts and the
layer; `annotation_moved` names the layer and, when the annotation's old
`/OC` was a membership rule rather than a layer, says the rule was replaced.

The refusals live with the other layer refusals in `text::panels::layeredit`
(`LayerRefusal::Assign*`).
