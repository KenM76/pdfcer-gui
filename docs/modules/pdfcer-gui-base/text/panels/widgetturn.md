# `text::panels::widgetturn` — the words for tilting a form field's box and its opacity

The Properties rows, the status lines and the refusals for `turn_widget` and
for a widget's `/CA`. Angles print to one decimal place only when they have a
fraction, with a degree sign, and always say *anticlockwise*: `/MK /R` and the
free angle are both counterclockwise in the file.
